## Notable Events of 2013

| Event | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| [Pope Francis Elected as 266th Pope]({{ '/news/people/pope-francis-elected/' | relative_url }}) | Cardinal Jorge Mario Bergoglio of Argentina was elected Pope on March 13, 2013, becoming the first pope from the Americas and the first from the Jesuit order, signaling a theological and geopolitical reorientation of the global Catholic Church. |
| [Harlem Shake Viral Phenomenon]({{ '/news/arts-culture/harlem-shake-viral-phenomenon/' | relative_url }}) | Dance meme exploded globally in February 2013, with 4,000+ videos uploaded daily at peak, reaching 1 billion views in 40 days and forcing Billboard to incorporate YouTube data into chart rankings. |
| [Edward Snowden's NSA Revelations]({{ '/news/history-politics/snowden-nsa-revelations/' | relative_url }}) | Former NSA contractor disclosed classified surveillance programs including PRISM and Tempora, sparking global debate on mass surveillance and government transparency. |
| [US Government Shutdown and Debt Ceiling Crisis]({{ '/news/history-politics/government-shutdown/' | relative_url }}) | Congressional gridlock over the 2014 budget and Affordable Care Act forced a 16-day government shutdown from October 1–17, furloughing 800,000 federal employees and costing $5 billion in economic losses. |
| [Boston Marathon Bombing]({{ '/news/history-politics/boston-marathon-bombing/' | relative_url }}) | Twin pressure-cooker bombs detonated near the Marathon finish line on April 15, killing three spectators and wounding at least 264 others, marking one of the deadliest terrorist attacks on U.S. soil since 9/11. |
| [Syria's Chemical Weapons Disarmament Agreement]({{ '/news/history-politics/syria-chemical-weapons-convention/' | relative_url }}) | Following the Ghouta chemical attack, the US, Russia, and Syria negotiated a disarmament framework, averting military intervention and establishing the first international inspections in an active conflict zone. |
| [Typhoon Haiyan Devastates Philippines]({{ '/news/society-economics/typhoon-haiyan-disaster/' | relative_url }}) | Super Typhoon Haiyan struck the Philippines on November 8 with 150+ mph winds, killing over 6,300 people and causing $2.99 billion in damage—the costliest typhoon in Philippine history. |
| [Bitcoin's Dramatic Price Surge]({{ '/news/society-economics/bitcoin-price-surge/' | relative_url }}) | Cryptocurrency surged from ~$13 to over $1,000, marking the first major speculative boom and demonstrating early blockchain technology volatility. |
| [The Great Gatsby Film Adaptation Release]({{ '/news/arts-culture/the-great-gatsby-film-release/' | relative_url }}) | Baz Luhrmann's visually maximalist adaptation of F. Scott Fitzgerald's classic opened in wide release on May 10, 2013, starring Leonardo DiCaprio and demonstrating the enduring cultural resonance of 1920s aesthetics and the American Dream's contradictions. |
| [Google Glass Launch]({{ '/news/science-technology/google-glass-launch/' | relative_url }}) | Google released its wearable augmented-reality glasses to limited users, pioneering the mainstream AR/wearables trend and sparking privacy concerns. |
| [CERN Confirms Discovery of Higgs Boson]({{ '/news/science-technology/higgs-boson-confirmation/' | relative_url }}) | CERN scientists confirmed the discovery of the Higgs boson at the Large Hadron Collider, validating 50 years of theoretical physics and earning the Nobel Prize in Physics. |
| [Quantum Entanglement Teleportation Breakthrough]({{ '/news/science-technology/quantum-entanglement-teleportation/' | relative_url }}) | Physicists in Germany, Switzerland, and Denmark teleported quantum states between stationary matter qubits—single atoms, superconducting circuits, and atomic ensembles—advancing the building blocks of quantum networks and quantum computing. |
| [Malala Yousafzai's Education Advocacy]({{ '/news/people/malala-yousafzai-education-advocate/' | relative_url }}) | After recovering from a Taliban attack, Malala founded the Malala Fund and became the youngest-ever Nobel Peace Prize nominee, championing girls' education globally. |
| [Hugo Chávez's Death and Legacy]({{ '/news/people/hugo-chavez-death-legacy/' | relative_url }}) | Hugo Chávez, the influential Venezuelan president who led a Bolivarian Revolution, died at 58 after a cancer battle, triggering an early election and shaping Latin American political discourse. |
| [Nelson Mandela's Death and Historic Legacy]({{ '/news/people/nelson-mandela-death-legacy/' | relative_url }}) | South Africa's first democratically elected president and anti-apartheid icon died at 95, leaving a transformative legacy as a symbol of reconciliation and human rights. |
| ['Selfie' Named Oxford Word of the Year]({{ '/news/arts-culture/selfie-oxford-word-of-year/' | relative_url }}) | Oxford Dictionaries selected "selfie" as Word of the Year, reflecting a 17,000% surge in usage and marking a cultural shift toward digital self-documentation and social sharing. |
